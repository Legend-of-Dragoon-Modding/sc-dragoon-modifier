===========================================================================================================================================================
Hard Mode Character Enhancements
===========================================================================================================================================================
Hard Mode character enhancements can be accessed through the Enhancement Icon when playing either Hard Mode.
Most of them are manual, some will trigger automatically. 

===============
= Dart        =
= Burn Stacks =
===============
Burn Stacks are gained by using Dragoon Physical or Magic attacks. 
Burn Stacks cap at a maximum of 15.
Each Burn Stack grants the Burn Stack attack an additional 10% damage.
When Burn Stacks are at the maximum of 15...
-Dart will temporarily heal all MP. 
-Flameshot will do the same damage as Final Burst, and Explosion will do the same damage as Red-Eyed Dragon.

Dart gains Burn Stacks based on the attack
Dragoon Physical - 1
Flameshot        - 1
Exploson         - 2
Final Burst      - 3
Red-Eyed Dragon  - 4
Divine DG Ball   - 3
Divine DG Cannon - 3

When Dart reachs the at or above the following Burn Stack amount he will heal MP.
 4 Burn Stacks - 10 MP
 8 Burn Stacks - 20 MP
12 Burn Stacks - 30 MP

After Dart uses a Burn Stack attack through the Enhancement Icon
-Fire Damage is nullified
-Water Damage is increased by 15%
-Other damage is decreased by 15%
until his next turn.

Burn Stacks are displayed at the lower bar of the character portrait.

==============================
= Lavitz/Albert              =
= Wind Mark and Wind Barrier =
==============================
Lavitz and Albert can apply Wind Marks to enemies by using Dragoon Magic attacks.
When an enemy has a Wind Mark applied and is attacked by allies their Turn Point Value is reduced by 30.
Enemies can have up to a maximum of 4 Wind Marks applied.

Wind Marks can not be stacked through attacks, when a Dragoon Magic is attack is successful it will set the follow amount of Wind Marks
Wing Blaster - 1
Gaspless     - 2
Jade Dragon  - 3

Lavitz and Albert thorugh the Enhancement Icon can activate the Wind Barrier at the cost of 10 MP per turn.
When they are attacked when Wind Barrier is active the following will happen
-Lavitz/Albert will heal 5 MP
-One Wind Mark will be added to the enemy

Wind Marks are displayed at the bottom bar of the character portrait.
Wind Barrier if active is display at the top bar of the character portrait.

===================
= Shana/Miranda   =
= Elemental Arrow =
===================
Shana's final total AT stat is multiplied in the following scenarios
x1.4 when equipping Detonate Arrow
If Detonate Arrow is not equipped
 x2.15 when level is above 28
 x1.9 when level is above 20
 x1.6 when level is above 10
Various locations in the game now drop Elemental Arrows and can be used during Shana/Miranda's turn via the secondary battle menu.
    Fire Arrow    - Volcano Villude          (36%)
    Dark Arrow    - Hellena Prision (Disc 4) (24%)  
    Water Arrow   - Undersea Cavern          (24%)
    Water Arrow   - Kashua Glacier           (24%)
    Wind Arrow    - Nest of Dragon           (24%)
    Wind Arrow    - Evergreen Forest         (24%)
    Earth Arrow   - Home of Giganto          (24%)
    Earth Arrow   - Kadessa                  (24%) 
    Dark Arrow    - Phantom Ship             (24%)   
    Dark Arrow    - Death City Mayfil        (24%)
    Dark Arrow    - Phantom Ship (Disc 4)    (24%)   
    Light Arrow   - Moon                     (24%)
    Light Arrow   - Shrine of Shirley        (24%) 
    Thunder Arrow - Vellweb                  (24%) 
    Thunder Arrow - Flanvel Tower            (24%) 
If a location does not have dedicated Elemental Arrow, a random Elemental Arrow will drop at 24%.
Shana/Miranda has a new mechanic called Elemental Field activated by Elemental Arrows throgh the Enhancement Icon.
When an Elemental Arrow is used Shana/Miranda will gain one Elemental Charge matching the Elemental Type of the Elemental Arrow for the Elemental Field. 
Once all three Elemental Charges are gained the Elemental Field activates and provides various effects for three of Shana/Miranda's turns. 
When the Elemental Field activates the next three arrows will contribute to Elemental Shift.
When the Elemental Field is activated one of the following will occur: 
	If three different elements were used to create the Elemental Field the damage for those elements is reduced by 12%.
	If two elements were the same to create the Elemental Field this element will have it's damage increased by 8% while the other is reduced by 4%.
	If all three elements used to create the Elemental Field were the same the damage for that element will be increased by 12%.
When Elemental Shift and Elemental Field have a total of six arrows the Element Shift effect will occur.
	If three different elements in the Elemental Shift are different than the three elements in the Elemental Field, Shana/Miranda gains the Elemental Prism effect.
		Elemental Prism - Shana/Miranda's next Elemental Arrow turns into the Prismatic Arrow casting a Psyche Bomb.
	If all elements in the Elemental Shift are the same as the ones in the Elemental Field and they match the element of one of the enemies on the field, Shana/Miranda gains the Elemental Overabundance effect.
		Elemental Overabundance - Shana/Miranda's next Elemental Arrow that matches the element of the target will deal 75% more damage. If the target is Thunder this damage is further increased by 50%.		
	If all elements in the Elemental Shift are the same as the ones in the Elemental Field, Shana/Miranda gains the Elemental Torrent effect.
		Elemental Torrent - Shana/Miranda's normal arrows have their damage infused by the matching element they do 250% more damage for 3 turns.
	If all elements in the Elemental Shift are the elemental opposite of the ones in the Elemental Field Shana/Miranda gains the Elemental Reaction effect.
		Elemental Reaction - Shana/Miranda's next Elemental Arrow will provided the Elemental Reaction effect on the targeted enemy for three turns that are activated upon hitting the enemy. The element based on the reaction must be used.
			Fire/Water - Vaporize   - The party will heal MP based on 0.8% of the damage dealt up to a maximum of 50 MP. 
			Wind/Earth - Turbulence - The party will gain SP based on 1% of the damage dealt up to a maximum of 100 SP. If the character is in Dragoon forms the amount of turns in Dragoon will recalculate.
			Dark/Light - Equilbirum - The party will heal based on 8% of the damage dealt.
	If there are three of the same element in the Elemental Shift and three of the same element but different than the ones in the Elemental Field, Shana/Miranda gains the Elemental Unity effect.
		Elemental Unity - Shana/Miranda's Elemental Arrow of the same element of the two elements of Elemental Unity will provide bonuses to one of those two party members if present in the party.
			Fire    - Dart will gain 1 Burn Stack per Fire Arrow.
			Water   - Meru and Shana/Miranda will regain 20 MP per Water Arrow. Meru will gain Ice Shield based on 10% of the damage dealt,
			Wind    - Wind Arrow will not consume Wind Marks on the enemy and instead restore 1 Wind Mark.
			Earth   - Earth Arrow will provide Protection Shield based on 10% of the damage dealt up to a maximum of 15% of Shana/Miranda's HP to Kongol.
			Dark    - Rose will gain Siphon based on 20% of the damage dealt. 
			Light   - Shana/Miranda will restore an additional 100 SP when Light Arrow is used and heal 5% of the damage dealt up to a maximum of 5% of Meru's HP.
			Thunder - Thunder Arrow has an additional chance to apply another Thunder Charge to the enemy.
	If none of the above conditions are met Shana/Miranda will temporarly not consume Elemental Arrows.
When Elemental Shift is finished the Elemental Cooldown state begins where no arrows can be added to Elemental Field or Elemental Shift.
When Elemental Cooldown is finished all Elemental Field and Shift arrows are cleared from the entire party.
Only one Elemental Field and one Elemental Shift can exist at time.

Elemental Arrows used are displayed below Shana/Miranda's section in the battle UI when attacking enemies on Shana/Miranda's turn..
Elemental Arrow ammo left is displayed on the bottom bar of the character portait.

================
= Rose         =
= Siphon Burst =
================
When Rose physically attacks an enemy or uses any attack in Dragoon she will convert a portion of the damage dealt to Siphon.
Rose will also heal a portion of the damage dealt.
Rose can store the amount of Max HP she has at the start of battle as Siphon.
Rose can activate Siphon Burst through the Enhancement Icon.
Once acitvated the next attack will have the amount of Siphon added to the damage.

The amount of Siphon stored is displayed on the bottom bar of the character portait.
When a Siphon Burst is activated it will be displayed on the top bar of the character portait.

==================================
= Haschel                        =
= Thunder Charge / Static Charge =
==================================
Haschel while in the party, any Thunder attack done by any ally may apply a Thunder Charge.
A monster can have a maximum of 10 Thunder Charges applied to them.
When a monster has 10 Thunder Charges and Haschel uses Dragoon Spark Net the damage of Dragoon Spark Net will be increased by
-3.5x if the monster is not Thunder Element
-13x + (Haschel's Dragoon Level) if the monster is Thunder Element
When a monster is hit by Dragoon Spark Net with 10 Thunder Charges the enemy will enter the Overcharged state for 7 turns.
In the Overcharged state Thunder attacks will deal more damage to the monster, and this is further increased if the monster was Thunder Element.

Haschel can transfer Thunder Charges between enemies by collecting them as Static Charges by activating it through the Enhancement Icon.
Haschel can carry 20 Static Charges at a time.
Haschel when attacking an enemy will apply Thunder Charges equal to the amount of Static Charges up to a maximum of 10.
Static Charges must be fully depleted.

The amount of Thunder Charges is displayed on the bottom bar of the character portait when attacking a single monster.
The amount of Static Charges is display on the top bar of the character portairt when attacking a single monster.

===========================
= Meru                    =
= Ice Shield/Wingly Magic =
===========================
Meru will gain 5 MP when using a physical attack, if she is in Dragoon this is increased to 20 MP.
Meru can activte Wingly Magic State through the Enhancement Icon, when she attacks she will gain Ice Shield.
Ice Shield is used to lower the amount of damage done to Meru. 
Wingly Magic State reduces the amount of MP per turn by 10.

The amount of Ice Shield is displayed on the bottom bar of the character portrait.
If the Wingly Magic State is active it is displayed on the top bar of the character portrait.

=================
= Kongol        =
= Counterstance =
=================
Kongol can activate the Counterstance through the Enhancement Icon.
While in this state Kongol will cast an enhanced version of Speed Down (66%) on himself for three turns.
Kongol will always be considered guarding, but damage is increased by 70%. 
Knogol will heal 5% HP when taking a turn.
Kongol will gain a full amount of Turn Points Value for a single turn when he is attacked.
Kongol can only attack in this state.
